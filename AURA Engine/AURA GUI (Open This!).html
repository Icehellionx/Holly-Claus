Shifts (advanced, optional)
Conditional sub-entries for this lore rule. Each shift is a “daughter” of the main entry.
Shifts for this entry
Click once to select a shift (for duplicate/delete). Double-click to open the Shift Editor.
How this works
Each shift is visually shown as a child under its parent entry in the left-hand list with a vertical bar. When you add or open a shift, a separate Shift Editor page lets you change priority, tags, and text.
Current: Entry editor. Shifts remain attached to whatever entry you have selected.
